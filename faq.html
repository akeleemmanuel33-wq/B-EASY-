Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about B-Easy 🇳🇬

What is B-Easy?

+

B-Easy is a simple business management tool designed specifically for Nigerian small businesses, shop owners, freelancers, and online sellers. It helps you create invoices, track sales, manage customers, and monitor your business performance - all from your phone!

Is B-Easy really free?

+

Yes! Our Free plan is completely free forever. You can create unlimited invoices, manage up to 50 customers, track daily sales, and use basic dashboard features at no cost. We also have a Pro plan (₦2,500/month) with advanced features like unlimited customers, auto payment reminders, and detailed analytics.

Do I need accounting knowledge to use B-Easy?

+

Not at all! B-Easy is designed to be simple and easy to use. No accounting degree or technical knowledge needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use B-Easy. Everything is explained in simple terms with clear visuals.

Can I use B-Easy on my phone?

+

Absolutely! B-Easy is mobile-first, meaning it's designed specifically to work perfectly on your phone. You can manage your entire business from anywhere, anytime. It also works great on tablets and computers.

How do I share invoices with my customers?

+

Creating and sharing invoices is super easy! Once you create an invoice, you can instantly download it as a PDF or share it directly on WhatsApp, email, or any messaging app. Your customers will receive a professional-looking invoice in seconds.

What's the difference between Free and Pro plans?

+

The Free plan includes unlimited invoices, up to 50 customers, sales tracking, and basic dashboard. The Pro plan (₦2,500/month) adds unlimited customers, automatic payment reminders via WhatsApp/SMS, advanced analytics, expense tracking, data export, staff accounts, and priority support. Start with Free and upgrade when you're ready!

Is my business data safe and secure?

+

Yes! We take security very seriously. Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your business information. Your data belongs to you, and we will never share it with third parties.

When will B-Easy be available?
